# Music_store_Analysis

This query finds the customer who has spent the most money in the store.
It pulls the customer's personal details along with the total amount they have paid.
To do that, it combines two tables – one with customer info and one with all their purchases.
It adds up every purchase for each customer individually to see their total spending.
Then it sorts everyone from highest spender to lowest and picks just the very top person.
